Our story

One Office, Both States.

River City Realty is a Fort Smith brokerage working the Arkansas River Valley and eastern Oklahoma. We are small on purpose. There is one office, on Rogers Avenue, and when you call it you reach someone who will still be your contact when the deal closes.

Being licensed in both states matters more here than most places. The line runs right through this market — a family relocating for a job at Fort Smith often ends up looking in Pocola or Poteau, and a seller in Van Buren regularly fields offers from buyers who work in Oklahoma. Our agents can represent you on either side of it without handing you off to somebody else's brokerage.

We handle residential, land, and commercial. We also do a good deal of work most brokerages in this market don't take on, which has taught us how to move a property that needs a specific kind of buyer rather than a general one. That experience shows up in how we price, how we photograph, and how quickly we can tell you what something is actually worth.

How we work

What You Can Expect From Us

Community First

We're not just working here — we live here. Our agents are involved in the communities they serve, which means they bring genuine local knowledge to every conversation, not just MLS data.

Honest Guidance

We take our fiduciary duty seriously. You'll get straight answers — not just what you want to hear. Our clients are confident that we prioritize their interests at every step of the transaction.

Trained and Current

Our brokers hold executive and principal licenses in both states and keep current on continuing education. When a question comes up mid-transaction, the answer is in the building.

Full Network

Real estate is a team sport. We work closely with local lenders, title companies, and industry professionals to make sure every transaction has the right people around the table.

Client for Life

We're not closing a deal and moving on. Our relationships with clients outlast the transaction — and in a market this size, that reputation is everything.

No Franchise Overhead

As an independent brokerage, we're not paying franchise fees or following a national playbook. That means our focus stays exactly where it belongs — on you and your transaction.
